Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or email us. In the game, Settings is the gear button on the title screen (and on the pause card during a level).

How does the purchase work?

On iPhone and iPad, levels 1–5 are free. One purchase, made once through the App Store, unlocks all the other levels for good. You will see the offer (Unlock all levels) when you reach level 6, and in Settings. On Android the game is free and every level is open.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. The same link is at the bottom of the purchase card that appears when you try a locked level. Make sure you are online and signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. The purchase belongs to your Apple ID, not to us.

I paid, but the levels are still locked.

Try Restore purchases first. If a payment is waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy), the levels unlock automatically once the App Store confirms it. Refunds are handled by Apple: support.apple.com.

How is my progress saved? Can I move to a new phone?

Automatically, on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, so your stars are not synced between devices. Your progress stays when you close or update the game, and is removed if you uninstall it or clear its data. On iPhone and iPad, setting up a new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one usually brings it along. Your purchase moves with your Apple ID: on the new device, install the game and tap Restore purchases.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in your device's language when it can. To change it, open Settings and tap Language. There are 18 languages to choose from.

I can't hear anything.

Open Settings and check that Sound and Music are switched on. Also check your device's volume and, on iPhone, the silent switch.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play. On iPhone and iPad, seeing the price, buying and restoring need a connection to the App Store. Once you own the game, it remembers that on your device, so you can keep playing offline. The Android version never uses the internet.
