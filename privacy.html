Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

This policy describes how Marble Gates (the “game”), published by YIWEN DENG (an individual developer, “we”), handles information. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the game and to this website.

Summary

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. Marble Gates has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and you can play it without an internet connection. The only online activity is on iOS, where the game asks the Apple App Store for the price and whether you own the full game, and where buying or restoring it goes through the App Store (see In-app purchase). The Android version has no purchase and does not use the internet at all.

What the game stores on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps these files in its private storage on your device:

  • your save: the stars you earned on each level, which tutorials you have seen, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, visual effects and the language you chose);
  • on iOS, whether you own the full game (a copy of the App Store's last answer, so the game works offline).

These files contain only game data, nothing that identifies you, and the game does not send them anywhere. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. On iOS, your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer backup) may include these files, according to your device settings; on Android, your phone's backup or transfer features may include them. Backups and transfers are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms, and we have no access to them.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports, and we do not sell or share personal data. The game contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. The only third-party code that uses the internet is Apple's StoreKit on iOS, which handles the purchase (see below).

Permissions

The Android version requests only vibration, used for light haptic feedback (you can turn it off in the game's settings). The iOS version does not ask for any permission. The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

In-app purchase

Marble Gates is free to download. On iOS, the first five levels are free, and a single one-time in-app purchase unlocks all the other levels. The purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details, your name or your Apple ID. The Android version has no in-app purchase.

To show the price and to know whether you own the purchase, the iOS game asks the App Store on your device, for example when the game starts, when you buy and when you choose “Restore purchases”. The App Store answers from your Apple ID, which needs an internet connection; the game talks only to the App Store, never to a server of ours. If the App Store cannot be reached, the game keeps its last answer (saved on your device), so you can keep playing offline. We do not run a server and keep no record of purchases. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your payment and purchase data.

Children

Marble Gates is a gentle puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. It has no advertising, no analytics, no chat or contact with other players and no links out of the game, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. On iOS the one purchase is made through the App Store, where Apple's parental controls (such as Ask to Buy) apply. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Third parties

You get the game through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which may collect information about your download, your purchases and your use of their store under their own policies (Apple, Google). We do not receive personal information from them about you. This website is static: it uses no cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or external fonts. The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which, like any web host, may process technical request data (such as your IP address) in its server logs under its own policy; we do not have access to that data in a way that identifies you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct, delete or export. Your save is on your device and under your control. Data about your purchase is held by Apple. Depending on where you live, you have rights over personal data, including under the EU and UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority), California's CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information), and Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI). To make any request or ask a question, email us at the address below; if we hold nothing about you, we will tell you so.

We process no personal data of players, so there is no legal basis to state under the GDPR; if you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and delete them when no longer needed. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not transfer it internationally.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data differently (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
